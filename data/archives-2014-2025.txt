How to read the Archives
ESPN years: the #1 row is the playoff champion, not the regular-season 1-seed. That is why Towner/Aga sit over two 10-3 teams in 2014 and Rymo is the 2020 champ at 6-7.
Fantrax years: the table is regular-season order by record then points for. Gold / silver / bronze still mark the playoff podium. Green-tint rows 4–8 made the 8-team field. 2024 and 2025 were 14-game seasons with no ties.
Sak retired after 2025. Jacob Crisp (Jacob's Jalopys) is the 2026 rookie in that chair.
